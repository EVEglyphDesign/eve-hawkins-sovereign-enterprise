The argument

Six moves, one asset

The asset never changes: the twin — the structural record of how Peterbilt Atlantic actually runs, held in a repository the dealer owns. What changes is what the twin is asked to carry.

Move one

Cannibalise the CRM spend

Every dollar of future CRM investment is redirected into the twin. The dealer stops renting access to its own sales pipeline. Peterbilt Atlantic does not run Salesforce today, so the argument is about money not yet committed.

Move two

Backup support for every dealer

The twin as the durable record behind whatever front end each dealer already runs. Nobody is asked to switch anything. Structure is taken from the backup and recovery system, never from production.

Move three

Value-capture pipeline sovereignty

What a vendor currently monetises — knowing the pipeline in a comparable shape across every dealer — becomes an asset on the dealers' own side of the table.

Move four

A unified system for individual owners

Each dealer contributes the component its own operation needs; components are shared across the network. An enterprise system that grows by contribution rather than by licence.

Move five

The pattern transfers to utilities

Utilities are being pushed to convert perpetual ECC licences into subscriptions at exactly the moment their structured data becomes sovereign. The structured data describing a power plant should be owned by the people it serves. That is a safety argument, not a cost argument.

Move six

The Global Copyright Umbrella

The instrument that makes contribution safe: it separates authorship from access, structure from data, and contribution from surrender. It is why a dealer can give a component away without losing it.

The honest ledger

What is not resolved

  • The guarantee against reuse. Tim asked twice for a guarantee that his data and the work built on it would not be used elsewhere. He received a proposed mitigation — Luke placed in direct control — and an offer to sign a contract. No contract has been executed. That is the true status as at the issue of this paper.
  • Manufacturer system access is asserted to be obtainable, not demonstrated. The PACCAR service contract is to be loaded and read before anything is touched.
  • The funding mechanics behind “max funded”, and who files the claims, were addressed conceptually rather than procedurally.
  • The CDK bolt-on subscription referred to as SalesDrive was not identified by anyone in the meeting. Its cost and function are unknown.
